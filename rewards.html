Got Questions?

Rewards Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about earning 1% credit on purchases of $25 or more.

A: Simply create your account on this page! Every time you order $25 or more in-store or online, you earn a 1% cash credit in real dollars and cents that you can use on future cakes, cookies, and pastries.
A: To qualify for the 1% rewards credit, an order must have a subtotal of $25.00 or higher. Purchases below $25.00 do not accrue rewards credit. Once you reach or exceed $25.00, you receive a full 1% credit in dollars and cents on the entire order amount!
A: It is simple arithmetic with no rounding tricks:
  • A $25.00 purchase earns $0.25 credit.
  • A $48.50 (8" Burnt Almond Cake) purchase earns $0.49 credit.
  • A $78.00 (10" Burnt Almond Cake) purchase earns $0.78 credit.
  • A $100.00 purchase earns $1.00 credit.
  • A $200.00 catering order earns $2.00 credit.
Any fraction of a cent is rounded to the nearest full cent.

When the app launches, all your registered email credentials and rewards balances will seamlessly carry over.
A: Absolutely! Your dollars-and-cents reward credit can be applied toward any bakery item, including our world-famous Burnt Almond Cake, Strawberry Rings, fresh morning danishes, fruit tarts, cream pies, and artisan breads.

Important Ordering Notice

Welcome to Peters' Bakery

If this is your first time to Peters’ Bakery, welcome. Online orders require a 48 hour lead time. Make sure to select your desired pickup date and time during checkout.

Heads Up on Lines & Pickup: We are a small business with a small lobby and only one main door. There is no separate line for pre-orders versus walk-in customers. On weekends and holidays, please prepare to arrive earlier than your desired pickup time.

When adequate staffing allows, we open a side door for pre-orders, though staffing shortages may sometimes prevent this. Thank you for stopping by our home!

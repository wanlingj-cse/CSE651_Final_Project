Got Questions?

Rewards Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know about earning 1% credit on purchases of $25 or more, the 90-day expiration, and the 7-day reminder.

A: Simply create your account on this page! Every time you spend $25 or more in-store or online, you earn a 1% cash credit in real dollars and cents recorded in our Supabase backend database, ready to redeem on future cakes, cookies, and pastries.
A: To qualify for the 1% rewards credit, an order must have a subtotal of $25.00 or higher. Purchases below $25.00 do not accrue rewards credit. Once you reach or exceed $25.00, you receive a full 1% credit in dollars and cents on the entire order amount!
A: All rewards credits expire in 90 days from the date of the qualifying purchase. This gives you three full months to redeem your credit toward your favorite treats!
A: Whenever you log into your rewards account, our backend system immediately scans your active credits. If any credit is within 7 days of its 90-day expiration date, a pop-up reminder window alerts you to redeem your credit before it expires!
A: Absolutely! Your rewards credit in real dollars and cents is directly redeemable on your very next bakery purchase. When ordering online at Peters' Bakery Online Ordering, your available rewards balance appears in your cart drawer. Simply check the "Redeem rewards credit on this purchase" toggle to deduct your savings from your order total! You can also redeem your credit in-store at counter checkout.
A: It is simple arithmetic with no rounding tricks:
  • A $25.00 purchase earns $0.25 credit.
  • A $48.50 (8" Burnt Almond Cake) purchase earns $0.49 credit.
  • A $78.00 (10" Burnt Almond Cake) purchase earns $0.78 credit.
  • A $100.00 purchase earns $1.00 credit.
  • A $200.00 catering order earns $2.00 credit.
Any fraction of a cent is rounded to the nearest full cent.
A: Absolutely! Your dollars-and-cents reward credit can be applied toward any bakery item, including our world-famous Burnt Almond Cake, Strawberry Rings, fresh morning danishes, fruit tarts, cream pies, and artisan breads.

Supabase Backend Status

Peters' Bakery Rewards records customer data and spends in PostgreSQL via Supabase. Credits expire in 90 days, and the system notifies customers upon login when credits expire in 7 days.

Database Schema: customers & customer_spends
Credit Validity: 90 Days from Purchase
Login Alert Trigger: Within 7 Days of Expiration
Schema definition file available at: supabase/schema.sql. Serverless handlers in: api/rewards-backend.js.

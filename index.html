Artisanal Specialties

What We Bake

At Peters’ Bakery, we’ve been baking unforgettable treats since 1936, using time-honored recipes and only the highest-quality ingredients. We’re best known for our iconic Burnt Almond Cake, but our menu offers much more—from fruit and cream pies like apple, berry, and coconut, to rich chocolate and lemon cream pies. Our pastry case is filled daily with fresh doughnuts, buttery danish, tender croissants, and whipped cream éclairs. We bake a wide variety of cookies and bars, including shortbread, chocolate chip, Mexican wedding cookies, brownies, and our famous fruit bars. Our breads range from sweet Portuguese loaves to banana nut, cinnamon swirl, and classic white. We also feature seasonal favorites like Strawberry Rings in spring and summer, Pumpkin Burnt Almond Cake in autumn, and festive holiday staples like Stollen and Pfeffernüsse.

Since 1936

The Story of Peters' Bakery

When you walk into Peters’ Bakery, you’re not just picking up a cake—you’re stepping into a living piece of San Jose history. Tony was born in 1907 to a humble family on a ranch in Berryessa. During the Great Depression, he spent weekdays working for Studebaker and weekends riding the train to San Francisco to master the craft of baking at his uncle’s shop.

“If you don’t want to eat it yourself, don’t sell it.”

— Tony Peters, Founder

On April 15, 1936, Tony opened the first Peters’ Bakery in downtown San Jose, later building our permanent home on Alum Rock Avenue in 1947. Today, Peters’ Bakery is still proudly owned and operated by Tony’s grandchildren—making it the oldest family-owned bakery in Silicon Valley.

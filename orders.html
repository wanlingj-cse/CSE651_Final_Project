Customer Advisory Notice

If this is your first time to Peters’ Bakery, welcome.

Online orders require a 48 hour lead time.

Make sure to select your desired pickup date and time during check out.

Small Lobby & Single Main Door

A friendly heads up to those of you who are new. We are a small business, with a small lobby and only one main door. There is no separate line for those with a pre-order versus those who are walking in.

Plan Ahead for Weekends & Holidays

If you are placing an order for a weekend or Holiday, please prepare to arrive earlier than your desired pickup time. There is usually a line out front and we want everyone to be prepared and arrive to their celebrations on time.

Pre-Order Side Door Triage

When we have adequate staffing, we will open a side door for pre-orders. However, due to staffing shortages and staff call-outs, this is not always possible. Thank you for stopping by our home!

Our Commitment to Quality

Return & Refund Policy

At Peters' Bakery, we take great pride in the quality and freshness of our baked goods. Each item is carefully crafted, and we want every customer to be delighted with their order.

1. Returns Required for Refunds or Credits

Because our products are freshly baked and perishable, we have the following policy in place:

  • Cancellation Window: No refund will be given if an order is cancelled within 24 hours of the scheduled pickup time.
  • 48-Hour Return Window: To be eligible for a refund, store credit, or replacement, the majority of the product must be returned to us within 48 business hours of purchase along with your original receipt.
  • Product Must Be Largely Intact: If only a small portion of the product is returned after most of it has been consumed or discarded, we cannot honor a refund or replacement. If a product truly does not meet expectations, it should be returned largely intact.
  • Quality Evaluation: Returning the item allows our master bakers to evaluate the product and understand what went wrong so we can continuously improve our craft.

2. Items Not Eligible for Refund

Unreturned Baked Goods: Products that are not physically returned cannot be refunded or credited. Unfortunately, we cannot make exceptions, as past misuse of our policy has made this requirement necessary.

Gift Cards: Electronic or physical gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be redeemed for cash.

Bakery Apparel & Shirts: All sales on t-shirts and apparel are final. Unlike a super-store that orders tens of thousands of shirts and can absorb losses on returns, we sustain a loss on every shirt returned because we cannot resell clothing that has already been worn and there is no way for us to verify if it has been worn or not. This policy ensures our customers never have to buy a shirt already worn by someone else.

3. Custom & Specialty Orders

Because of the custom labor, special ingredients, and preparation time required, custom cakes or large specialty orders may only be refunded if returned within the guidelines above. Wedding cakes require a 2-week cancellation notice.

Unclaimed Orders & Charity Donation: If you forgot to pick up your order, please call us that same day if possible. We will keep all orders for one day, after which the fresh product will be donated to local charity to avoid food waste. If your order was for Saturday, please call before end of business if you would like it kept for Tuesday pickup.

4. How to Request a Refund or Credit

Please return the product along with your sales receipt to the bakery within 48 business hours at 3108 Alum Rock Ave, San Jose, CA 95127.

If you believe you did not receive an electronic gift card that was sent, please contact us directly at (408) 258-3529 so we can look into the matter immediately.

Our goal is always to provide the best possible experience. If you are ever unsatisfied, we encourage you to reach out to us directly. We greatly value your feedback—it helps us continue to serve our community with the quality and care you expect from Peters' Bakery!
